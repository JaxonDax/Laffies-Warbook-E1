========================================================================================
                    CORPORATE TIER CAPS & STAFF EXPERIENCE RULES
========================================================================================
Your company is restricted by its current scale. To push your attributes higher and 
unlock sophisticated manufacturing techniques, you must invest in infrastructure upgrades.

[ 📈 STAT CAPS BY COMPANY TIER ]
Your Tier dictates the absolute maximum limit for your operational attributes:
 👉 TIER I   : Attribute Stats are capped at a maximum of  5 / 5
 👉 TIER II  : Attribute Stats are capped at a maximum of 10 / 10
 👉 TIER III : Attribute Stats are capped at a maximum of 15 / 15

[ 👥 STAFF EXPERIENCE (XP) & PROMOTIONS ]
Your company's efficiency grows through the baseline competence of your crew.
 ├─ Automatic Gain : The staff automatically gains +1 XP at the end of every turn loop.
 ├─ GM Bonus XP     : The GM may award an extra +1 Staff XP for defensive combat victories.
 └─ Breakthroughs   : At 5 Staff XP, the crew hits an operational milestone. Reset 
                      XP to 0 and claim one Milestone Reward:
                      👉 Add +1 to any Stat (Up to your current Tier Cap).
                      👉 Save the Breakthrough toward a permanent Company Tier upgrade.

[ 🚀 UPGRADING COMPANY TIER ]
To scale up your entire operations, you must spend saved Breakthroughs and pay an expansion tax:
 ├─ Upgrade to Tier II : Costs 3 Saved Breakthroughs AND 150,000 Manna.
 └─ Upgrade to Tier III: Costs 3 Saved Breakthroughs AND 500,000 Manna.

========================================================================================
                     THE CORPORATE DOWNTIME LOOP (PHASES 1 - 4)
========================================================================================
[FLEET BASELINE CAPACITY: Sum of all owned ship Max Gross Yield values in Manna]

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📌 PHASE 1: EXTRACTION PROTOCOL (The Harvest)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The player rolls 1d100 and adds the company's Extraction Modifier: (OPERATIONS x 5).

*NOTE: The total roll CAN exceed 100% to reflect zero-waste mining or rare veins.*
 ├─ Under 100% : Standard yield percentage of the fleet's baseline capacity.
 └─ Over 100%  : Over-Extraction! Your crew found rare crystalline isotopes or achieved 
                 perfect extraction efficiency, generating a bounty past peak capacity.
                 (e.g., A total roll of 115 = 115% of the fleet's baseline capacity)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📌 PHASE 2: HAZARD EVALUATION (The Danger)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The GM rolls 1d100 and SUBTRACTS the fleet's Defense Modifier: (SECURITY x 5).

 [Modified Roll] | SEVERITY        | IMPACT ON PHASE 1 BASE T0 ORE
 ----------------+-----------------+----------------------------------------------------
  01 - 70        | Clear Skies     | 0% Ore Loss. Operations run smoothly.
  71 - 85        | Minor Incident  | 15% Ore Loss. (Equipment strain / clogged lines)
  86 - 95        | Major Crisis    | 40% Ore Loss. (Structural damage / Raider skirmish)
  96 - 100+      | Catastrophic    | 80% Ore Loss. *TRIGGER MECH COMBAT ENCOUNTER*
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 *GM Note: If players deploy mechs and win the Catastrophic battle, they recover the lost ore!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📌 PHASE 3: MANUFACTURING & FACTORY PRODUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Route raw ore through the factory decks to increase its value. All production is instant.
👉 PRODUCTION INVESTMENT CAP: Max ore sent to machines cannot exceed (CAPITAL x 50,000 Manna).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📌 PHASE 4: THE MARKET & LIQUIDATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Distribute your cargo safely to long-term contract clients (gated by INFLUENCE), and 
dump any excess volume onto the uncontracted public open market.

========================================================================================
               PHASE 4 LEDGER: REGULAR CLIENT CONTRACTS & THE OPEN MARKET
========================================================================================
Your INFLUENCE stat determines how many Active Contract Slots your company maintains.
(1 Slot per point of Influence). You may allocate cargo safely to Contracts before 
rolling leftover inventory on the Open Market.

[ 🤝 CONTRACT CATALOG ]
Choose which regular clients fill your open slots. You can swap contracts at the 
beginning of any downtime cycle.

 TIER     | CLIENT CORPORATE NAME      | CONTRACT LIMITATION & GUARANTEED PAYOUT RATE
 ---------+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------
  TIER I  | Colonial Logistics Corp    | Buys up to 40,000 Manna of T0/T1 at locked 1.05x
          | Local Heavy Industry       | Buys up to 30,000 Manna of T1/T2 at locked 1.10x
 ---------+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------
  TIER II | Regional Militia Fleet     | Buys up to 75,000 Manna of T2/T3 at locked 1.15x
          | Sub-Sector Tech Conglom.   | Buys up to 60,000 Manna of T3/T4 at locked 1.20x
 ---------+----------------------------+------------------------------------------------
  TIER III| Interstellar Mega-Corp     | Buys up to 150,000 Manna of T4/T5 at locked 1.25x
          | Karrakin Trade Baron       | Buys up to 100,000 Manna of ANY at locked 1.35x

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ 🌐 THE PUBLIC OPEN MARKET (RAW 1d100 - NO MODIFIERS) ]
Dumping remaining cargo onto uncontracted public open exchanges. Roll a flat 1d100.

 [d100 Roll] | MARKET STATUS      | PRICE MODIFIER | NARRATIVE IMPACT
 ------------+--------------------+----------------+------------------------------------
  01 - 10    | Market Crash       | x 0.75         | Sudden tariff spikes or sector panic.
  11 - 25    | Minor Market Dip   | x 0.90         | Temporary local over-supply.
  26 - 85    | STABLE DEMAND      | x 1.00         | Standard baseline galactic value.
  86 - 95    | High Demand        | x 1.15         | Local colony experiencing growth.
  96 - 100   | Extreme Scarcity   | x 1.30         | Supply lines cut; desperation buy.
